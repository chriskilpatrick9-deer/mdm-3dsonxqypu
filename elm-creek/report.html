Weather and deer activity

Sighting and harvest rates compared with each hunt day’s temperature, wind, pressure, and moon. These comparisons are observational.

  • Over 6 hunt days with at least 8 hunter-hours, hunters saw 17.4 deer per 100 hours and recorded 68 harvests in 821 sits. With only 6 hunt days, these patterns are suggestive, not conclusive.
  • Days with rising pressure (Oct 16, Oct 18, and Nov 21–22) and days with falling pressure (Oct 17 and Nov 20) had similar sighting and harvest rates (16.4 per 100 hours and 40 harvests in 528 sits (7.6 per 100 sits) vs 19.0 per 100 hours and 28 harvests in 293 sits (9.6 per 100 sits)).
  • Calmer days (Oct 17–18 and Nov 21, about 9–11 mph) and windier days (Oct 16, Nov 20, and Nov 22, about 12–14 mph) had similar sighting rates (16.1 vs 18.5 per 100 hours) but different harvest rates: 27 harvests in 415 sits (6.5 per 100 sits) vs 41 harvests in 406 sits (10.1 per 100 sits).
  • Sits that started in the morning had 15.5 per 100 hours and 44 harvests in 522 sits (8.4 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 48°F. Afternoon sits had 23.4 per 100 hours and 24 harvests in 299 sits (8.0 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 55°F.
  • Days near a full moon (Nov 20–22, 83–96% lit) had 16.1 per 100 hours and 28 harvests in 391 sits (7.2 per 100 sits), compared with 19.8 per 100 hours and 35 harvests in 305 sits (11.5 per 100 sits) on days closer to a new or crescent moon (Oct 16–17).
  • The highest sighting rate was on Oct 16 (20.5 per 100 hours, 20 harvests, 35–52°F, NNW at 12.2 mph, rising pressure).

Notes

  • Hunt days are Oct 16–Nov 22, as listed on the MBRB hunt page. Stand-setup days (Oct 15, Nov 19) and any sits on other dates are not in the statistics.
  • Sightings are logged as a single total for each sit at check-out, not timestamped. AM and PM rates use when the sit started (AM = in before noon). Rates marked * have fewer than 50 hunter-hours.
  • Harvest entries include wounded deer; Recovered means the app status is “Recovered”. A harvest is dated from the hunter’s matching sit when the logged date has no sit; otherwise the date is as entered in the app.
  • Weather is hourly Open-Meteo data at the park centre. The weather analysis covers hunt days only (no setup days) and is observational — with few days it is suggestive, not conclusive.
