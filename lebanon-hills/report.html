Weather and deer activity

Sighting and harvest rates compared with each hunt day’s temperature, wind, pressure, and moon. These comparisons are observational.

  • Over 6 hunt days with at least 8 hunter-hours, hunters saw 19.8 deer per 100 hours and recorded 35 harvests in 369 sits. With only 6 hunt days, these patterns are suggestive, not conclusive.
  • Colder days (Nov 23–25, lows 17–25°F) and warmer days (Nov 16–18, lows 34–44°F) had similar sighting and harvest rates (19.4 per 100 hours and 16 harvests in 183 sits (8.7 per 100 sits) vs 20.2 per 100 hours and 19 harvests in 186 sits (10.2 per 100 sits)). The two sets fell in different hunt periods and also differed in rising versus falling pressure, so these factors cannot be separated from temperature.
  • Sits that started in the morning had 20.4 per 100 hours and 30 harvests in 241 sits (12.4 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 37°F. Afternoon sits had 17.8 per 100 hours and 5 harvests in 128 sits (3.9 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 44°F.
  • The highest sighting rate was on Nov 16 (27.4 per 100 hours, 12 harvests, 34–55°F, SSW at 9.9 mph, falling pressure).

Notes

  • Hunt days are Nov 16–18 (1st period); Nov 23–25 (2nd period), as listed on the MBRB hunt page. Stand-setup days (Nov 15, Nov 22) and any sits on other dates are not in the statistics.
  • Sightings are logged as a single total for each sit at check-out, not timestamped. AM and PM rates use when the sit started (AM = in before noon). Rates marked * have fewer than 50 hunter-hours.
  • Harvest entries include wounded deer; Recovered means the app status is “Recovered”. A harvest is dated from the hunter’s matching sit when the logged date has no sit; otherwise the date is as entered in the app.
  • Weather is hourly Open-Meteo data at the park centre. The weather analysis covers hunt days only (no setup days) and is observational — with few days it is suggestive, not conclusive.
