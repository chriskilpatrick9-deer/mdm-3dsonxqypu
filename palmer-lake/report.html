Weather and deer activity

Sighting and harvest rates compared with each hunt day’s temperature, wind, pressure, and moon. These comparisons are observational.

  • Over 9 hunt days with at least 8 hunter-hours, hunters saw 26.6 deer per 100 hours and recorded 14 harvests in 106 sits. With only 9 hunt days, these patterns are suggestive, not conclusive.
  • The colder days (Oct 5, Oct 19–20, and Nov 4, lows 39–48°F) had 23.8 per 100 hours and 6 harvests in 49 sits (12.2 per 100 sits), compared with 32.0 per 100 hours and 8 harvests in 43 sits (18.6 per 100 sits) on the warmer days (Oct 6–7, Oct 21, and Nov 2, lows 45–58°F).
  • Days with rising pressure (Oct 7, Oct 19, and Nov 4) had 23.5 per 100 hours and 3 harvests in 35 sits (8.6 per 100 sits), compared with 31.4 per 100 hours and 8 harvests in 49 sits (16.3 per 100 sits) on days with falling pressure (Oct 5–6, Oct 20, and Nov 2). 2 days had steady pressure.
  • Calmer days (Oct 5 and Oct 19–21, about 2–5 mph) had 22.5 per 100 hours and 9 harvests in 47 sits (19.1 per 100 sits), compared with 33.9 per 100 hours and 4 harvests in 47 sits (8.5 per 100 sits) on windier days (Oct 6 and Nov 2–4, about 7–13 mph).
  • Sits that started in the morning had 23.1 per 100 hours and 10 harvests in 51 sits (19.6 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 54°F. Afternoon sits had 32.2 per 100 hours and 4 harvests in 52 sits (7.7 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 64°F.
  • The highest sighting rate was on Oct 6 (57.6 per 100 hours, 3 harvests, 58–82°F, SW at 7.3 mph, falling pressure).

Notes

  • Hunt days are Oct 5–7 (1st period); Oct 19–21 (2nd period); Nov 2–4 (3rd period), as listed on the MBRB hunt page. Stand-setup days (Oct 4, Oct 18, Nov 1) and any sits on other dates are not in the statistics.
  • Sightings are logged as a single total for each sit at check-out, not timestamped. AM and PM rates use when the sit started (AM = in before noon). Rates marked * have fewer than 50 hunter-hours.
  • Harvest entries include wounded deer; Recovered means the app status is “Recovered”. A harvest is dated from the hunter’s matching sit when the logged date has no sit; otherwise the date is as entered in the app.
  • Weather is hourly Open-Meteo data at the park centre. The weather analysis covers hunt days only (no setup days) and is observational — with few days it is suggestive, not conclusive.
  • Hunt A and Hunt B are hunter groups (who hunted), not separate areas of the park. The map shows the park as one area.
