Weather and deer activity

Sighting and harvest rates compared with each hunt day’s temperature, wind, pressure, and moon. These comparisons are observational.

  • Over 6 hunt days with at least 8 hunter-hours, hunters saw 14.9 deer per 100 hours and recorded 26 harvests in 487 sits. With only 6 hunt days, these patterns are suggestive, not conclusive.
  • Days with rising pressure (Sep 27) had 7.9 per 100 hours and 1 harvest in 87 sits (1.1 per 100 sits), compared with 14.3 per 100 hours and 18 harvests in 324 sits (5.6 per 100 sits) on days with falling pressure (Sep 25–26 and Oct 31–Nov 1). 1 day had steady pressure.
  • Calmer days (Sep 27 and Oct 30–31, about 3–6 mph) and windier days (Sep 25–26 and Nov 1, about 6–9 mph) had similar sighting and harvest rates (14.8 per 100 hours and 10 harvests in 241 sits (4.1 per 100 sits) vs 15.0 per 100 hours and 16 harvests in 246 sits (6.5 per 100 sits)).
  • Sits that started in the morning had 12.8 per 100 hours and 17 harvests in 282 sits (6.0 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 56°F. Afternoon sits had 19.5 per 100 hours and 9 harvests in 205 sits (4.4 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 62°F.
  • The highest sighting rate was on Oct 30 (23.3 per 100 hours, 7 harvests, 36–58°F, ESE at 5.3 mph, steady pressure).

Notes

  • Hunt days are Sep 25–27 (1st period); Oct 30–Nov 1 (2nd period), as listed on the MBRB hunt page. Stand-setup days (Sep 24, Oct 29) and any sits on other dates are not in the statistics.
  • Sightings are logged as a single total for each sit at check-out, not timestamped. AM and PM rates use when the sit started (AM = in before noon). Rates marked * have fewer than 50 hunter-hours.
  • Harvest entries include wounded deer; Recovered means the app status is “Recovered”. A harvest is dated from the hunter’s matching sit when the logged date has no sit; otherwise the date is as entered in the app.
