Weather and deer activity

Sighting and harvest rates compared with each hunt day’s temperature, wind, pressure, and moon. These comparisons are observational.

  • Over 6 hunt days with at least 8 hunter-hours, hunters saw 17.8 deer per 100 hours and recorded 18 harvests in 274 sits. With only 6 hunt days, these patterns are suggestive, not conclusive. 1 listed hunt day had too few hours to include.
  • Colder days (Nov 13–15, lows 20–26°F) and warmer days (Oct 2–4, lows 40–53°F) had similar sighting rates (17.6 vs 18.0 per 100 hours) but different harvest rates: 12 harvests in 123 sits (9.8 per 100 sits) vs 6 harvests in 151 sits (4.0 per 100 sits). The two sets fell in different hunt periods, so season or period may be involved, not weather alone.
  • Days with rising pressure (Oct 2, Oct 4, and Nov 14) had 20.6 per 100 hours and 5 harvests in 140 sits (3.6 per 100 sits), compared with 11.5 per 100 hours and 3 harvests in 53 sits (5.7 per 100 sits) on days with falling pressure (Oct 3). 2 days had steady pressure.
  • Calmer days (Oct 2 and Nov 13–14, about 2–4 mph) had 22.4 per 100 hours and 10 harvests in 141 sits (7.1 per 100 sits), compared with 12.3 per 100 hours and 8 harvests in 133 sits (6.0 per 100 sits) on windier days (Oct 3–4 and Nov 15, about 6–9 mph).
  • Sits that started in the morning had 14.2 per 100 hours and 10 harvests in 164 sits (6.1 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 42°F. Afternoon sits had 26.7 per 100 hours and 8 harvests in 110 sits (7.3 per 100 sits), with sit-time temperatures averaging 53°F.
  • The highest sighting rate was on Oct 2 (26.6 per 100 hours, 3 harvests, 40–63°F, NNW at 2.7 mph, rising pressure).

Notes

  • Hunt days are Oct 2–4 (1st period); Nov 13–16 (2nd period), as listed on the MBRB hunt page. Stand-setup days (Oct 1, Nov 12) and any sits on other dates are not in the statistics.
  • Sightings are logged as a single total for each sit at check-out, not timestamped. AM and PM rates use when the sit started (AM = in before noon). Rates marked * have fewer than 50 hunter-hours.
  • Harvest entries include wounded deer; Recovered means the app status is “Recovered”. A harvest is dated from the hunter’s matching sit when the logged date has no sit; otherwise the date is as entered in the app.
  • Weather is hourly Open-Meteo data at the park centre. The weather analysis covers hunt days only (no setup days) and is observational — with few days it is suggestive, not conclusive.
